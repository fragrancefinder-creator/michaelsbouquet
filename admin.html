Every time the quiz shows someone their results, the fragrances shown get logged here — anonymously, no personal data. This tells you what people are actually matching with most.

Resetting permanently deletes all logged results (for every period). Use this if you want to start counting from zero — e.g. at the start of a new season or promo. This can't be undone.
